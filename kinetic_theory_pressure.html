The Molecular Origin of Pressure

A box of idealized, non-interacting gas molecules, bouncing off the walls. Every strike hands the wall a little momentum — add those impulses up over time and divide by the wall length, and pressure falls straight out. Adjust the molecule count, temperature, and volume below and watch the gauge follow the kinetic theory prediction in real time.

Each dot is a molecule of unit mass, moving in a straight line until it strikes a wall and reflects elastically. No molecule–molecule collisions — this is the textbook ideal-gas idealization.
