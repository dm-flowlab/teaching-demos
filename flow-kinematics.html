Fluid Parcel Kinematics

Type a two-dimensional velocity field. SymPy derives the velocity gradient, strain rate, spin, divergence and vorticity exactly. A square fluid parcel is carried by the full flow in the large plate, and its local motion is split into translation, rotation, elongation and shear in the four plates below.

Adding the four rates gives the linearized velocity near the center, u ≈ u₀ + (D + W)·ξ, where ξ is the position relative to the center. Each mode plate is that one term's exact flow map: shift by u₀t, rotation by Ωt, stretching by exp(ε_xx t) and exp(ε_yy t), and the shear map with cosh(ε_xy t) and sinh(ε_xy t).

  
The large plate integrates particle paths through the full field with fourth-order Runge–Kutta (240 frames, 3 substeps each), so the parcel bends where the flow is nonuniform. The dotted outline is the exact flow map of the linearized field. Fields are steady.
