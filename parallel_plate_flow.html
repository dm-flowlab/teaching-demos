Flow Between Parallel Plates

Steady, fully developed laminar flow between two infinite plates inclined at θ to the horizontal, driven by any combination of plate motion, a pressure gradient along the plates and gravity. The profiles come from the exact plane Couette–Poiseuille solution, computed in Python running in your browser.

    Derivation

    Assumptions: steady, incompressible, laminar flow of a Newtonian fluid with constant μ and ρ between infinite plates; fully developed, so ∂u/∂x = 0, continuity gives v = 0 and u = u(y). The y-momentum equation, 0 = −∂p/∂y − ρg cos θ, makes the pressure hydrostatic across the gap, so dp/dx is the same at every y. The x-momentum equation then reduces to an ODE that SymPy integrates below.
